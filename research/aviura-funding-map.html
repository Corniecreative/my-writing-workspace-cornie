Three moves to make first

01 · MALAYSIA · TAX

MIDA New Incentive Framework

Up to 100% ITA

Live since 1 March 2026. A new line can earn an Investment Tax Allowance of up to 100%, or a special tax rate as low as 0%, for up to 15 years. Open to foreign-owned companies.

02 · MALAYSIA · GUARANTEED LOAN

GTFS 5.0 through a Malaysian bank

Closes 31 Dec 2026

Government guarantees 60–80% of the green part of the financing (energy-efficient machinery, waste reduction). Applied for through participating banks.

03 · MALAYSIA · SOFT LOAN + GRANT

MIDF soft loan and SAG MADANI grant

RM20m loan · RM1m grant

MIDF lends up to RM20m for machinery and automation. SAG MADANI matches automation spend 70:30, up to RM1m. Both require at least 51% Malaysian ownership.

Who does what

Finance

  • Confirm the Malaysian entity's ownership split. It decides whether MIDF and SAG MADANI are open to us.
  • Request a MIDA pre-assessment for the new line under the New Incentive Framework.
  • Get GTFS 5.0 terms from two participating banks before the 31 Dec deadline.

Operations

  • Final equipment list with supplier quotes. Every application asks for it.
  • Energy and waste figures for the new machinery, to size the green portion for GTFS.

Technical

  • One-page line specification: capacity, grades to run, automation level. Funders score automation and technology level.

Marketing

  • Letters of intent from two or three customers for the new line's output. Banks weigh these heavily.
  • Short Aviura profile for funder meetings.
