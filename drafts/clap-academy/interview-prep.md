## 2. Answer bank

### A. Business development

**Q1 — Find 20 organisations (UK or Nigeria)**
- **Criteria:** 200+ staff; sector under AI pressure (financial services, professional services, telecoms, energy, public sector); a *trigger signal* (AI/data job ads, new Chief Digital/Data Officer, digital transformation announcement, AI policy news); evidence of training budget; any warm connection.
- **Tools:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator (headcount, growth, job changes), Companies House / FCA register (UK), CAC search and BusinessDay / Nairametrics / ministry sites (Nigeria), job boards for AI-related hiring, ChatGPT/Claude to summarise annual reports into a one-page brief.
- **Output:** a scored list in the CRM (fit × trigger × access), top 20 get personalised outreach.
- **Your evidence:** at Aviura you research prospective manufacturers after exhibitions and organise contacts for targeted outreach.

**Q2 — Find the decision-maker and get the meeting**
- Map the buying group: *economic buyer* (CEO/COO, Permanent Secretary), *champion* (Head of L&D, HR Director, CDO), *gatekeeper* (procurement).
- Warm path first: mutual connections, events, alumni, UKAI network. Then a 3-touch sequence: LinkedIn engagement → personalised email with one specific insight → call.
- Ask for a 20-minute discovery call, not a sale: "Can I share what a similar organisation did in their first 90 days of AI adoption?"

**Q3 — A lead you generated (STAR)**
- **S:** Invited to present at ICES Cake Expo Nigeria.
- **T:** Turn a speaking slot into business, not just applause.
- **A:** Built the talk around attendees' real problems (AI for costing, planning); clear call to action; captured contacts; followed up personally. [add: how you captured leads — QR, form, WhatsApp?]
- **R:** 15+ enquiries → 3 paid workshop sign-ups.

**Q4 — UK organisation vs Nigerian government ministry**
| | UK corporate | Nigerian ministry |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | LinkedIn, email, L&D/HR | Relationships, formal letters, introductions, protocol |
| Buyer | Head of L&D / CHRO / COO | Permanent Secretary, Director HR/Training, plus procurement |
| Proof they want | ROI, productivity, regulation (FCA, data protection) | Alignment with national digital/AI agenda, credibility, track record with government |
| Cycle | Weeks to months | Months; tied to budget cycle |
| Paperwork | Supplier onboarding, possibly Procurement Act 2023 for public bodies | Vendor registration and compliance documents per Public Procurement Act 2007 |
- Your edge: you've worked in both cultures.

**Q5 — 30 contacted, 2 responses**
Diagnose in order: **list** (right org, right person, right timing?) → **message** (about them or about us? specific trigger?) → **channel** (email only? cold vs warm?) → **follow-up** (only one touch?). Test one change at a time, like the A/B testing you ran at Seed Mentors (+12% engagement).

### B. Marketing and positioning

**Q6 — Credible AI transformation partner, not "another training company"**
- Sell **outcomes**, not courses: "AI readiness for your workforce in 90 days."
- Proof assets: case studies with numbers, executive testimonials, the London government programme.
- **Thought leadership:** founder on LinkedIn, an executive podcast, a short "AI readiness index" or checklist as a lead magnet.
- A **diagnostic first** offer (readiness assessment → tailored programme), like the pilot-first approach you use at Traze.

**Q7 — A campaign you ran (STAR)**
Pick one: AI readiness webinar (objective, promotion channels, registrations, outcome) **or** Angel Analytics segmentation (ROI +40%). Have both ready.

**Q8 — LinkedIn content for HR/L&D, CEOs, public-sector leaders**
- HR/L&D: skills-gap data, "how to roll out AI training without fear," delegate stories.
- CEOs: productivity and risk, short executive clips, "3 decisions boards are making about AI."
- Public sector: service delivery, digital government, photos from the London programme (with consent).
- Formats: carousels, 60-second clips, founder posts, comment-led engagement. Evidence: Aviura LinkedIn grew 150 → 252 in under 9 months for a *niche B2B* product.

**Q9 — One 3-day programme → 5 more clients**
Case study (problem → programme → results) · 3–5 testimonial clips · photo story · LinkedIn series · one-page PDF for sales emails · "lookalike" outreach to similar organisations · ask the client for 2–3 introductions.

**Q10 — Real business metrics vs vanity metrics**
- **Vanity:** impressions, likes, follower count.
- **Business:** qualified leads, discovery calls booked, proposals sent, pipeline value (£), conversion rate, cost per qualified lead, time to close, referrals/repeat business.
- Tie every post to a next step (book a call, download, register) and track it in the CRM. You built Tableau/Excel KPI dashboards at Angel Analytics — offer to build Clap's weekly BD dashboard.

### E. Scenarios

**Scenario 1 — UK financial services firm, 500 staff, no AI training**
- Research: annual report, FCA register, LinkedIn (who runs L&D, data, operations), job ads, news.
- Likely needs: safe AI use (data protection, FCA expectations), productivity for operations staff, leadership understanding.
- **First email (draft):**
  > **Subject:** AI skills at [Firm] — a quick idea
  >
  > Hi [Name],
  >
  > I noticed [Firm] is [hiring for data roles / recently announced X]. Many financial services teams we speak to have the tools but no structured way to help staff use them safely and well.
  >
  > Clap Academy runs practical AI programmes for executives and teams, from leadership briefings to hands-on workshops, built around your regulatory context.
  >
  > Would a 20-minute call be useful to share how similar firms are approaching their first 90 days?
  >
  > Best, Cornelia
- Follow-up: Day 4 LinkedIn connect + comment · Day 8 email with a short useful resource · Day 15 call · Day 25 polite close-the-loop email.

**Scenario 2 — Nigerian federal ministry**
- Stakeholders: Minister's office, Permanent Secretary, Director of Human Resources/Training, ICT/digital director, procurement unit. Check links to the national digital/AI agenda.
- **Organisational brief:** mandate, current digital initiatives, leadership, recent news, how an AI readiness programme supports their goals.
- Contact: formal letter + warm introduction (alumni, professional bodies, diaspora networks), followed by a meeting request; respect protocol and timelines.
- **Procurement:** confirm vendor registration and the documents needed under the Public Procurement Act; align the proposal with the budget cycle; prepare the capability statement, past performance (the London programme), and a clear costed proposal. Ask the procurement unit early — don't guess.

**Scenario 3 — 25-employee lead, silent 3 weeks**
Value-add touch (relevant case study) → different channel (call/LinkedIn) → offer a smaller first step (pilot cohort, free 30-min readiness check) → try a second contact in the organisation → polite "should I close the file?" email. Log everything in the CRM. Never chase with "just checking in."

**Scenario 4 — London executive programme for senior government officials**
Get permissions first (officials' images need sign-off). Then: press-style announcement, photo story, short highlight video, 1–2 testimonial clips, case study PDF, founder LinkedIn post. Ask delegates for referrals; approach similar ministries/agencies with "we recently trained senior officials from…".

**Scenario 5 — 30 / 60 / 90 days** (targets to agree with the CEO)
- **Days 1–30 — Learn and set up:** learn the programmes, pricing and past clients; define target customer profiles for the UK and Nigeria; build a CRM with 100+ researched prospects; create 3 core assets (capability statement, case study, outreach templates); set up a weekly reporting dashboard.
- **Days 31–60 — Outreach:** 150+ personalised touches; 10+ discovery calls; regular LinkedIn content calendar running; first executive podcast/video recorded.
- **Days 61–90 — Convert:** 4–6 proposals submitted; 1–2 new clients or pilots; referral programme live; review what worked and set Q2 targets.

## 6. Tricky questions

- **"Will you keep running Traze?"** → Prepare an honest answer: e.g. it's a small side project / you'd pause it / no conflict because it focuses on [bakery/SME workflows], not corporate training. Decide this before the interview.
- **"You've moved roles often."** → Each role added a skill (SEO → product marketing → analytics → brand), and all of it comes together in this role.
- **"Experience selling to government?"** → Be honest. Point to stakeholder work with directors, research skills, Healthwatch public-sector exposure, and your understanding of Nigerian business culture.
