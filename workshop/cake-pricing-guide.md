# How a cake price is built

Every cake price is made of the same eight building blocks. If any block is missing, the price is too low.

| # | Block | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sponge** | Cake batter ingredients |
| 2 | **Filling** | Buttercream, jam, ganache, curd, cream cheese, fruit, etc. between layers |
| 3 | **Covering** | Outside finish: buttercream, ganache, fondant, whipped cream |
| 4 | **Decorations** | Toppers, sugar flowers, fresh flowers, sprinkles, gold leaf, edible prints |
| 5 | **Structure & packaging** | Cake board / drum, dowels, box, ribbon, labels |
| 6 | **Labour** | Your time at every stage, including admin and cleaning |
| 7 | **Overheads** | Your share of gas, electricity, data, transport, equipment wear |
| 8 | **Profit** | What the business earns *on top of* paying you for your time |

> **Total cost** = Sponge + Filling + Covering + Decorations + Structure & packaging + Labour + Overheads
>
> **Selling price** = Total cost + Profit % (then round up)
>
> **Extras charged separately:** delivery, set-up, rush orders.

---

# Step 1 · Describe the cake (the order details)

You can't price a cake until you know exactly what it is. Collect these details for every order:

| Detail | Options |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Round · Square · Rectangle/sheet · Heart · Number/letter · Custom carved |
| **Size** | 6" · 8" · 10" · 12" (or sheet size, e.g. 9×13") |
| **Height** | Standard (about 4", 3 layers) · Tall (about 6", 4 layers) · Double barrel (about 8") |
| **Tiers** | Single tier, or list each tier (e.g. 10" + 8" + 6") |
| **Sponge flavour** | Vanilla · Chocolate · Red velvet · Fruit cake · Carrot · etc. |
| **Filling** | Buttercream · Jam · Ganache · Curd · Cream cheese · Fresh fruit · Combination |
| **Covering** | Buttercream · Ganache · Fondant · Whipped cream · Semi-naked |
| **Decoration level** | Simple · Medium · Elaborate (see Step 5) |
| **Servings needed** | Party portions or wedding portions (see Portion Chart) |
| **Date & time needed** | Is it a rush order? |
| **Delivery** | Pick-up or delivery? Location? Set-up needed? |

---

# Step 2 · Work out the unit cost of every ingredient

AI can do this for you, but you must understand it:

> **Unit cost = Pack price ÷ Pack size**
>
> **Cost used = Unit cost × Amount used**

*Example:* Butter, 500g for ₦5,000 → ₦5,000 ÷ 500 = **₦10 per gram**. You used 450g → 450 × ₦10 = **₦4,500**.

Always use **your real prices** from receipts or your supplier, not what other bakers charge.

---

# Step 3 · Cost each component for your base cake

Write down your recipe for one **base cake: 8" round, standard height (3 layers)**. Cost each part separately (sponge, filling, covering) so you can mix and match.

*Why separate components?* A vanilla sponge with ganache filling and fondant covering costs very differently from a vanilla sponge with jam and buttercream. Costing each component once means you can price any combination quickly.

**Tip:** Cost frostings and fillings **per kilogram**, then multiply by how much the cake needs. For example, if your buttercream costs ₦5,650 per kg and the cake uses 700g, that's ₦5,650 × 0.7 = ₦3,955.

---

# Step 4 · Scale for size, shape and height

Once you know your base cake (8" round, standard height), use these factors to scale **ingredient costs** for other cakes.

**Size factor (round cakes, compared with 8")**

| Size | Factor |
|---|---|
| 6" round | × 0.56 |
| 8" round | × 1.00 (base) |
| 10" round | × 1.56 |
| 12" round | × 2.25 |

**Shape factor**

| Shape | Factor |
|---|---|
| Round | × 1.00 |
| Square (same size) | × 1.27 (a square has more cake than a round of the same width) |
| Heart, number or carved | Use the nearest square size, then add extra time for carving |

**Height factor**

| Height | Factor |
|---|---|
| Standard (about 4", 3 layers) | × 1.0 |
| Tall (about 6", 4 layers) | × 1.5 |
| Double barrel (about 8") | × 2.0 |

> **Ingredient cost for any cake = Base ingredient cost × Size factor × Shape factor × Height factor**

**Tiered cakes:** price each tier separately, then add them together. Add extra for dowels, central support and stacking time.

**Note:** these factors are for ingredients. Labour does **not** scale the same way. A 12" cake doesn't take 2.25 times as long to decorate, so estimate time separately (Step 6).

---

# Step 5 · Decorations

Cost decorations item by item, or use your own level guide.

| Level | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | Piped borders, sprinkles, a printed topper, one colour |
| **Medium** | Drip, macarons or chocolates on top, fresh flowers, acrylic topper, 2–3 colours |
| **Elaborate** | Handmade sugar flowers, hand-painting, figurines, gold leaf, edible prints, detailed themes |

Include anything bought for this cake (toppers, flowers, gold leaf), plus gel colours and lustre dust used.

---

# Step 6 · Labour (your time)

List every stage, not just the decorating.

| Stage | Example hours (8" standard) |
|---|---|
| Consultation, quoting, messages | 0.5 |
| Baking (mixing, baking, cooling) | 1.5 |
| Filling, stacking, crumb coat | 1.0 |
| Covering (final coat / fondant) | 1.0 |
| Decorating | 1.0 |
| Cleaning up | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **5.5 hours** |

> **Labour = Total hours × Your hourly rate**

*Your hourly rate:* what you'd pay a skilled baker to do this work. If you don't pay yourself, the business isn't really making money; it's using your free time.

The example times are only a guide. Time yourself on your next few cakes and use your real numbers.

---

# Step 7 · Overheads (your share of running costs)

> **Overheads per cake = Monthly overheads ÷ Number of cakes you make per month**

Include gas or electricity, data and phone, transport to buy supplies, cleaning supplies, and equipment wear (mixer, tins, turntable).

*Example:* ₦60,000 per month ÷ 20 cakes = **₦3,000 per cake**.

---

# Step 8 · Profit and the final price

> **Profit = Total cost × Profit %** (for example 30%)
>
> **Selling price = Total cost + Profit**, then **round up** to a clean number.

**Then check:**

- **Price per portion:** Selling price ÷ servings. Does it make sense for your market?
- **Minimum order price:** Set a lowest price you'll accept for any cake, so small orders are still worth your time.

**Extras (charge separately):**

| Extra | Suggested approach |
|---|---|
| Delivery | Set fees by distance or area |
| Set-up (e.g. weddings) | Flat fee |
| Rush order (under 72 hours) | Add 15–25% |
| Deposit | Ask for 50% to confirm the date |

# Worked Example

*These are example numbers, not real prices.*

**Order:** 8" round, standard height (3 layers), vanilla sponge, strawberry jam + vanilla buttercream filling, buttercream covering, medium decoration, about 20 party portions.

**1. Sponge (3 layers)**

| Ingredient | Pack price | Unit cost | Used | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flour | 2kg for ₦4,000 | ₦2/g | 450g | ₦900 |
| Sugar | 1kg for ₦2,000 | ₦2/g | 450g | ₦900 |
| Butter | 500g for ₦5,000 | ₦10/g | 450g | ₦4,500 |
| Eggs | 30 for ₦6,000 | ₦200 each | 8 | ₦1,600 |
| Milk | 1L for ₦3,000 | ₦3/ml | 120ml | ₦360 |
| Baking powder | 100g for ₦1,000 | ₦10/g | 15g | ₦150 |
| Vanilla | 100ml for ₦3,000 | ₦30/ml | 10ml | ₦300 |
| **Sponge total** | | | | **₦8,710** |

**Buttercream cost per kg:** butter 400g (₦4,000) + icing sugar 600g (1kg for ₦2,500 → ₦1,500) + vanilla 5ml (₦150) = **₦5,650 per kg**

**2. Filling**

| Item | Calculation | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Strawberry jam | 500g jar ₦3,500 → ₦7/g × 150g | ₦1,050 |
| Vanilla buttercream | 300g × ₦5.65/g | ₦1,695 |
| **Filling total** | | **₦2,745** |

**3. Covering**

| Item | Calculation | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Vanilla buttercream | 700g × ₦5.65/g | **₦3,955** |

**4. Decorations (medium)**

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Gel colours | ₦300 |
| Sprinkles | ₦700 |
| Acrylic topper | ₦2,500 |
| **Decorations total** | **₦3,500** |

**5. Structure & packaging**

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| 10" cake drum | ₦1,500 |
| Cake box | ₦2,000 |
| Ribbon / label | ₦300 |
| **Total** | **₦3,800** |

**6. Labour:** 5.5 hours × ₦2,500 = **₦13,750**

**7. Overheads:** ₦60,000 ÷ 20 cakes = **₦3,000**

**Summary**

| Block | Cost |
|---|---|
| Sponge | ₦8,710 |
| Filling | ₦2,745 |
| Covering | ₦3,955 |
| Decorations | ₦3,500 |
| Structure & packaging | ₦3,800 |
| Labour | ₦13,750 |
| Overheads | ₦3,000 |
| **Total cost** | **₦39,460** |
| Profit (30%) | ₦11,838 |
| **Selling price** | **₦51,298 → round up to ₦51,500** |
| Price per party portion (20) | about ₦2,575 |

**What this shows:** ingredients are only ₦15,410 of the cost. If a baker charged ₦25,000 because "the ingredients cost ₦15,000", they would be working for free and losing money on overheads.

**Scaling the same cake to 10" round:** ingredients (sponge + filling + covering) ₦15,410 × 1.56 = ₦24,040. Then re-check decorations, a bigger board and box, and extra time.

# Facilitator only: Calculator build spec (Day 2)

*Do not share this before Day 2.* This is the structure the Day 2 build should follow.

**Settings (entered once):**
- Currency (₦ / £)
- Hourly rate
- Monthly overheads and cakes per month (to work out overheads per cake)
- Default profit %
- Rush fee %, deposit %, delivery fees by area

**Ingredient library (entered once, reused):**
- Name, pack size, unit (g / ml / each), pack price, with unit cost worked out automatically

**Component recipes (built from the ingredient library):**
- Sponges, fillings and coverings, each costed for the base cake (8" round, standard height), or per kg for frostings

**New quote (for each order):**
1. Order details: shape, size, height, tiers, sponge, filling, covering, servings, date, delivery
2. Ingredient costs scaled automatically with size, shape and height factors
3. Decorations: add items or pick a level
4. Structure and packaging items
5. Labour hours by stage
6. Output: cost breakdown table, total cost, profit, rounded selling price, price per portion, deposit due, and a ready-to-send quote message for the customer

**Keep version 1 simple:** single-tier cakes first. Add tiers, saved quotes and order history later.
