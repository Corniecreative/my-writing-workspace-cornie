Day 1: Organise & Find Your Starting Point

Copy each box below and paste it into ChatGPT or Claude when we get to that step.
Session 1 of 3
Before we start: log in to ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) or Claude (claude.ai) on your laptop or phone. Keep this handout open.

1Set up your Bakery Project

A Project is a folder where AI always remembers your bakery. Create a new Project called “My Bakery Business”, then paste this into its instructions and fill in the brackets.

About my bakery:
- Business name: [ ]
- Location and currency: [e.g. Lagos, ₦ / London, £]
- What I sell: [e.g. celebration cakes, cupcakes, small chops]
- My customers: [e.g. birthdays, weddings, corporate]
- How I get orders: [WhatsApp, Instagram, referrals]
- I work: [alone / with help], [full-time / part-time]
- My biggest challenge right now: [ ]
Always give simple, practical answers for a small home bakery.
Ask me questions if you need more information.

2Take your AI Readiness Check

Start a new chat inside your Project and paste this. Answer each question from 1 to 5.

Act as an AI business coach for my bakery. Ask me these 5 questions
one at a time, and I'll answer each from 1 (not at all) to 5 (fully):
1. I know exactly what each cake costs me to make.
2. I keep records of my sales and expenses.
3. I post about my business consistently.
4. I have a weekly routine for orders, baking and follow-ups.
5. I already use AI tools in my business.
Then add up my score out of 25, tell me my level
(5–11 Getting Started, 12–18 Building, 19–25 Ready to Grow),
and tell me the ONE area I should fix first and why.

3Sort your weekly tasks

List 5 tasks you do every week (e.g. replying to enquiries, pricing, posting, shopping, bookkeeping), then paste:

Here are 5 tasks I do every week: [list them].
Sort each one into: ELIMINATE (stop doing), AUTOMATE (AI can help),
DELEGATE (someone else can do it), or KEEP (only I should do it).
Show it as a table with a one-line reason for each,
then tell me which AUTOMATE task would save me the most time or money.

📝 AI not working? Use paper

Score the 5 questions in Step 2 yourself (1–5 each) and add them up.
5–11 Getting Started · 12–18 Building · 19–25 Ready to Grow
Type your total in the Zoom chat.

🎂 Homework before Day 2

Pricing Recipe Card for one signature cake:
  • Each ingredient: pack size, pack price, amount used
  • Packaging & decoration costs
  • Hours spent · monthly overheads · your hourly rate
  • Create a free Lovable account (lovable.dev)

🔒 Never paste customer phone numbers, addresses or payment details into AI. Always double-check AI's numbers before quoting a price. Use your real supplier prices, not competitors' prices.
