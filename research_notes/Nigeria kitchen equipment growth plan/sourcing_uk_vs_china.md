# Sourcing UK Overstock/Liquidation vs China for a Small Nigerian Kitchen & Baking Equipment Importer

Research date: 9 Oct 2026. Method: web search (about 20 searches/fetches). Several direct page fetches failed (DNS errors on goodhopefreight.com, r-zoneenterprises.com, wholesaleclearance.co.uk), so some figures come from search-result snippets of those pages, not full reads. Most freight and duty figures come from forwarders' and brokers' marketing pages, not official tariffs. Treat them as indicative.

FX reference: NFEM official rate ₦1,331.50/USD on 7 Oct 2026; parallel market about ₦1,362/USD — [TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/dollar-to-naira-naira-slips-to-133290-at-official-market-strengthens-to-1357-parallel). ## 1. How UK excess stock flows, and what a small overseas buyer can realistically access

### Takeaway
UK excess stock moves in tiers. Big retailers (Amazon, Argos and others) sell returns and overstock through gated auction platforms such as B-Stock, which charge buyer's premiums and favour bulk, registered business buyers. Smaller buyers usually buy second-hand from UK pallet resellers and auction houses (Wholesale Clearance UK, Stocklear/Joblot, Simon Charles auctions, eBay job lots) at a mark-up. Charity channels such as In Kind Direct are closed to resale. A small Nigerian importer can realistically use the reseller/auction tier, ideally through a UK-based agent or relative who collects, inspects and consolidates.

### Cited Findings
- B-Stock runs Amazon's official liquidation auctions through dedicated storefronts. Buyers must register, and auction wins carry buyer's premiums, so the winning bid is not the full cost — [Closo, "Are Amazon Pallets a Real Thing? 2026"](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/are-amazon-pallets-a-real-thing) (US-focused guide).
- Amazon FBA Liquidations overstock pallets carry manifest retail values of $3,000–$8,000, and bids typically land at **8–15% of retail** (US figures) — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/are-amazon-pallets-a-real-thing).
- Retailer B2B auction model (US analogue): Best Buy sells pallets and truckloads of new and customer-returned kitchen appliances to registered bidders — [B-Stock / Best Buy](https://bstock.com/bestbuy/kitchen/).
- Stocklear (joblot.stocklear.co.uk) lists multi-pallet lots of kitchen-appliance customer returns (example: 7 pallets of Cdiscount kitchen returns, quality B) — [Stocklear auction 473](https://joblot.stocklear.co.uk/auction/473).
- UK auction houses sell mixed household returns pallets. Example: Simon Charles lot of about 142 assorted items, RRP £1,600, including a Salter press chopper and a deep fryer — [Simon Charles](https://simoncharles.com/lots/5022233/pallet-of-approximately-142-assorted-items-to-include-rrp-1600).
- Wholesale Clearance UK sells fixed-price returns and clearance pallets by job lot (some listed at £1,600 per job lot) — [Wholesale Clearance UK](https://www.wholesaleclearance.co.uk/pallet-offers.htm) (seen in search snippet only; the page fetch failed).
- Charity channel: In Kind Direct's recipient charities agree not to sell or trade donated goods, and the charity vets organisations to prevent resale. It charges charities a handling fee of about 20% of the original retail price — [Wikipedia: In Kind Direct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Kind_Direct). Similar US programmes (e.g. Kars4Kids) also bar selling, trading or passing goods on — [Kars4Kids](https://fargate.prod.kars4kids.org/in-kind-donation/join.php).

### Inferences
- The cheapest tier (8–15% of RRP) goes through first-tier retailer auctions. These favour UK-registered businesses that can collect from UK warehouses within short windows. An overseas buyer without a UK entity or agent will mostly pay second-tier reseller prices (often 20–45% of RRP; see Q2).
- TK Maxx, Dunelm and John Lewis: I found no public information on their liquidation channels. Off-price retailers like TK Maxx are themselves buyers of brand overstock, so they are a sink for excess stock, not a source.
- Charity-donated stock (In Kind Direct, Good360-type programmes) is legally and ethically off-limits for resale. Leave it out of the sourcing plan entirely.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether B-Stock UK storefronts (Amazon UK, Argos and others) accept non-UK buyers, or what their VAT/registration rules are. The pages were not retrievable.
- I found no data on Gekko, Clearance Pallets, ukjoblots, Bulk Buy Clearance or Merkandi pricing or export terms in this session.
- No information on Argos, Dunelm, John Lewis or TK Maxx disposal partners.

## 2. Typical prices: pallets, % of RRP, grades, untested-returns risk, kitchen brands

### Takeaway
Rough bands: first-tier auction about 8–15% of RRP (US data); graded B returns from UK/EU resellers about 40–45% of RRP. Untested returns carry real defect risk and, for some brands, recall risk (e.g. Tower air fryers). I found very few sourced UK pallet prices specific to kitchen brands.

### Cited Findings
- Stocklear kitchen-appliance customer-returns lot (grade B, 7 pallets) selling at **about 43% of RRP** — [Stocklear](https://joblot.stocklear.co.uk/auction/473).
- Amazon overstock pallets at **8–15% of manifest retail** (US) — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/are-amazon-pallets-a-real-thing).
- General resale heuristic by condition: Very Good about 60–80% of new value; Good about 40–60%; Acceptable about 20–40% (not official B-Stock grades) — [Nexscope](https://www.nexscope.ai/blog/amazon-liquidation-how-to-buy-return-pallets).
- Single-unit UK data points: Russell Hobbs saucepan set, customer returned, like new, about £54 on eBay — [eBay listing](https://www.ebay.de/itm/326835544656). Used, tested Salter XL 15L air fryer oven, £50 plus postage — [eBay](https://www.ebay.de/itm/286771654666).
- New-retail benchmarks: Russell Hobbs kettles from about £29, toasters about £19.99–£40 — [PriceSpy UK](https://pricespy.co.uk/s/russell-hobbs-kitchen-appliances/).
- Recall risk: OPSS recall of four Tower air fryer models (T17023, T17061BLK, T17067, T17087), made Jan 2022–Apr 2023, which can overheat and ignite — [UK Gov recall PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68387e1bc99c4f37ab4e86a5/2503-0094-product-recall-tower-air-fryer.pdf).
- Comparison point: a Thai exporter offers mixed kitchen-appliance pallets (blender, mixer, microwave, air fryer, kettle, toaster, coffee machine, rice cooker) at "US$2.50 unit price", MOQ 1 pallet. This is an unclear basis and likely untested or returns stock — [B2Brazil](https://b2brazil.com/hotsite/bbbinternational/premium-kitchen-appliances-pallet-assort).
- Several "UK pallets" pages found are keyword-stuffed spam (e.g. sourceforge/wiki pages) and are excluded as unreliable — e.g. [example](https://npc-for-vcmp.sourceforge.io/wikiw/index.php/20_Insightful_Quotes_On_Buy_Pallets_UK).

### Inferences
- For planning, use **35–45% of RRP** as the landed-in-UK cost for graded B returns bought from resellers, and **15–25%** for untested mixed returns. These are inferred from the figures above, not directly sourced for UK kitchen lots.
- Untested returns mix fully working items, cosmetic damage, missing parts (mixer bowls, beaters, air-fryer baskets) and dead units. A small importer must budget for a write-off or repair share. I found no sourced UK defect-rate figure.
- Any Tower air-fryer lot must be checked against the recall model numbers. Exporting recalled goods to Nigeria creates safety and legal exposure.
- Brands commonly seen in UK lots (Tefal, Prestige, Salter, Kenwood, Russell Hobbs, Tower, Swan) are mid-market. Kenwood stand mixers are the most valuable baking SKU, but I found no sourced pallet price for them.

### Gaps
- No sourced UK pallet prices for Kenwood/KitchenAid stand mixers, Tefal cookware sets, or Prestige/Swan items.
- No sourced UK defect or "dead on arrival" rates for untested kitchen returns.

## 3. Electrical compatibility (UK vs Nigeria)

### Takeaway
They are compatible. Nigeria uses 230V/50Hz, the same as the UK, and Type G (UK 3-pin) is the official standard. Type D sockets and universal sockets also exist. UK-plug appliances work in Nigeria without a converter; some outlets may need an adapter.

### Cited Findings
- Nigeria: 230V, 50Hz; Type G is the official standard; about half of households and hotels have universal sockets that accept G and C — [WorldStandards (updated Jul 2025)](https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plug-voltage-by-country/nigeria/).
- Nigeria uses Type D and G sockets at 230V/50Hz — [ixigo](https://www.ixigo.com/travel-guide/plug-and-adapter/nigeria).
- UK Type G plug standard — [Netio glossary](https://www.netio-products.com/en/glossary/type-g-uk-electrical-socketelectrical-plug).

### Inferences
- This is a real advantage for UK stock over EU-plug stock (e.g. French Cdiscount returns, which have Type E/F plugs). Chinese suppliers can fit UK plugs on request, so the plug is not a decisive advantage for UK over China.
- Grid instability and voltage surges in Nigeria are a separate durability issue for any appliance source. Not researched here.

### Gaps
- I found no SON standard text specifying plug or voltage rules for imported appliances.

## 4. Shipping UK to Nigeria: groupage, per kg/CBM, containers, transit, clearing, duties, SONCAP

### Takeaway
UK-to-Lagos diaspora cargo firms price sea freight **by weight, about £2–£3.50/kg**, with minimums and handling fees, and take 6–8 weeks. This is expensive for heavy goods (stand mixers, cookware). I found no sourced UK-origin 20ft container rate. Nigerian clearing of a 20ft costs a few hundred thousand naira in terminal, shipping-line and agent fees, plus demurrage risk. Duty is set by the ECOWAS CET (bands 0/5/10/20/35%) plus VAT 7.5% and levies. I could not confirm the exact HS-line rates for cookware or appliances. SONCAP applies to regulated products including electricals: Product Certificate $500–$2,000 plus SONCAP Certificate $350 per shipment.

### Cited Findings — UK to Lagos freight
- R-Zone Cargo (2026 guide): sea freight from **£3/kg all-inclusive**, weekly sailings; a 20kg box about £60–90 — [R-Zone](https://r-zoneenterprises.com/blog/sea-freight-uk-to-nigeria) (snippet only; the fetch failed).
- Hezelinks: from **£3.50/kg**, plus a £25 handling fee, 50kg minimum — [Hezelinks](https://hezelinks.com/sea-freight-cargo-shipping-from-uk-to-nigeria/).
- A-B Cargo Express: **£2.50/kg** to Lagos by sea, 30kg minimum, **6–8 weeks** — [A-B Cargo Express](http://www.abcargoexpress.com/a-bce/).
- CargoAfrik: general goods **£2.00/kg** by sea to Lagos, 50kg minimum, £20 handling; sails from Tilbury roughly monthly — [CargoAfrik](https://cargoafrik.co.uk/price-list-of-cargo-to-nigeria/).
- Cargonaija (2025, older): sea from **£1.70–£2.50/kg** — [Cargonaija](https://cargonaija.com/cost-of-shipping-from-uk-to-nigeria-in-2025-updated-guide/).
- Air: Virgin Atlantic Cargo tariff (effective 29 Mar 2026), Lagos express: minimum 485 plus 13.90/kg up to 100kg (airline leg only) — [Virgin Atlantic Cargo](https://www.virginatlanticcargo.com/content/dam/cargo/pdf/BOStariff.pdf). Courier: 1kg parcel to Lagos from £36.50 plus VAT — [Transglobal Express](https://www.transglobalexpress.co.uk/parcel-to/lagos/).
- LCL pricing convention: charged per revenue tonne (1 CBM or 1,000kg, whichever is greater), plus fixed documentation/CFS fees. A 20GP starts to beat LCL at about 15 CBM — [Sino-Shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/freight-china-nigeria/) (China lane, but the convention is general).

### Cited Findings — Nigeria port/clearing
- Car-in-20ft clearing bundle (May 2026): Tin Can about ₦280k–450k, Apapa about ₦260k–420k. Terminal handling ₦150k–250k; shipping-line release ₦80k–150k; documentation ₦20k–50k; agent fee ₦200k–400k; demurrage ₦25k–60k/day after free days — [Awajis](https://awajis.com/cost-of-clearing-cars-in-nigeria/) (vehicles, not general cargo).
- 20ft demurrage N48,000/day (date unclear) — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/?p=1201500).
- Haulage of a 20ft from Apapa to Alaba is at least N400,000 (older report) — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/business-services/maritime/how-multiple-checkpoints-compound-haulage-costs-at-apapa-port/).
- Agents complained of over ₦3.5m consolidator charges on one 20ft groupage consignment, showing destination-side groupage cost risk — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/business-services/maritime/clearing-agents-brokers-decry-n3-5m-charges-on-20ft-container/).
- Overview of container costs out of Apapa in 2026 — [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/05/31/how-much-it-costs-to-ship-a-container-out-of-apapa-in-2026/) (not read in full).

### Cited Findings — duties
- Nigeria applies the ECOWAS CET five bands: **0%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 35%**. 20% is generally consumer/finished goods; 35% is protected-sector goods. An Import Adjustment Tax applies to 192 tariff lines in 2026 — [DHL Nigeria guide](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-ng/logistics-advice/essential-guides/importing-to-nigeria-).
- Add-ons: **VAT 7.5%**, **CISS 1%**, port development levy — [Deepbeez](https://deepbeez.com/import-duty/customs-nigeria); **ECOWAS levy 0.5%** — [SGK Global](https://sgkglobal.com/nigeria-customs-import-regulations-2026/).
- Nigeria keeps supplemental levies that significantly raise effective rates on selected imports — [US trade.gov CCG](https://www.trade.gov/knowledge-product/nigeria-import-tariffs).

### Cited Findings — SONCAP and used electricals
- SONCAP fees (from 1 Aug 2022, still current per BV Oct 2024): **Product Certificate $500 unregistered / $1,000 registered / $2,000 licensed; SONCAP Certificate $350 per shipment**. Testing is extra. An unregistered PC is valid 6 months, per shipment; registered and licensed PCs last 1 year — [Cotecna](https://cotecna-global.net1-01.test.voxteneo.com/media/news/implementation-of-new-nigeria-soncap-fee-structure); [Bureau Veritas Rev 8.4 (Oct 2024)](https://cdn2-verigates.bureauveritas.com/sites/verigates/files/2024-10/NIGERIA%20%28SONCAP%29%20Rev%208.4.pdf). This conflicts with SGS's 2020 datasheet ($300/$300/$1,300) — [SGS](https://www.sgsonline.com.cn/static/upload/2020/03/31/sgs%20pca%20nigeria%20datasheet%20a4%20en%20v21.pdf).
- Regulated products arriving without a SONCAP certificate can be detained, tested and possibly destroyed at the importer's expense — [SON FAQ](https://son.gov.ng/helpie_faq/why-do-i-need-soncap-certificate-for-my-import-to-nigeria/).
- Used EEE is not banned, but importing end-of-life or damaged equipment (e-waste) is illegal; NESREA admits used equipment only under functionality/compliance conditions. Fine ₦5m or up to 2 years' imprisonment. About a quarter of studied used-EEE imports were non-functioning — [Al Jazeera, Mar 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/3/27/truly-junk-e-waste-from-rich-nations-floods-local-markets-in-nigeria). Shipping non-working electronics breaches the Basel Convention and EU waste-shipment rules — same source.

### Inferences
- **Per-kg pricing penalises UK sourcing of heavy kitchenware.** Example: a 6–8kg boxed stand mixer at £2.50–3.50/kg costs about £15–28 per unit (₦27k–50k) in freight alone. For bulky-but-light items (air fryers about 5–6kg) it is about £12–21. A 20ft or 40ft FCL from a UK port would be much cheaper per unit, but only if volume justifies it (more than about 15 CBM).
- Pricing for small kitchen appliances: 20% CET plus 7.5% VAT (charged on CIF plus duty) plus about 1.5–2% levies gives roughly **30–32% of CIF** in total taxes. This rests on the *assumption* that these HS lines are in the 20% band and not on the IAT list. Verify with a broker for HS 8509 (food mixers/blenders), 8516 (fryers/ovens/kettles), 7323 (steel cookware) and 7615 (aluminium cookware).
- **Untested UK returns are a legal grey zone at both ends.** Non-working electricals can count as waste under Basel and NESREA rules. Test and grade items in the UK before export, and ship only working units.
- SONCAP adds a fixed per-shipment cost (at least $350 SC plus a PC). This hurts small, frequent UK consignments of mixed brands more than repeat China orders of the same SKU, where a registered PC covers a year.

### Gaps
- No sourced UK port (Felixstowe/Tilbury/London Gateway) to Lagos 20ft/40ft FCL rate for 2026.
- No sourced per-CBM UK groupage rate (UK diaspora firms quote per kg).
- Exact Nigerian CET duty rates for HS 7323, 7615, 8509, 8516 not confirmed from the official NCS tariff.
- UK-side export rules for used or untested electricals (Environment Agency WEEE/waste-shipment guidance) not researched.
- Whether diaspora per-kg cargo firms handle SONCAP/Form M for commercial quantities is unclear. Many seem to bundle clearing "all-inclusive", which may mean informal clearing.

## 5. China route: prices, freight, MOQs, quality

### Takeaway
China unit costs are far lower. Factory air fryers sell at about ¥95–218 on 1688 (about US$13–31) or US$21–37 FOB on export platforms. Home stand mixers cost US$39–65 FOB, with MOQs of 500–2,000 on export sites. China-to-Lagos sea LCL is mostly US$85–195/CBM (one outlier at $435). A 20GP costs about $2,970–3,630 in Sept/Oct 2026. Transit is about 35–50 days. Agents and 1688 consolidators lower MOQs for small buyers, but quality varies by factory.

### Cited Findings — product prices
- 1688 air fryers about **¥95–218**; a Midea model about ¥113 with bulk purchase available (undated) — [1688 wiki](https://wiki.1688.com/en/kitchen-appliances/WKfxunsiyw4lxc).
- Made-in-China air fryers about **US$21–37/unit FOB**, MOQ 520–1,000 pcs — [Made-in-China](https://m.made-in-china.com/hot-china-products/Air_Fryer.html).
- Home stand mixers about **US$39–65 FOB**, MOQ 500–2,000 — [Made-in-China](https://www.made-in-china.com/products-search/hot-china-products/Wholesale_Stand_Mixer.html); one at US$51.50–55.90, MOQ 1,000 — [Made-in-China](https://www.made-in-china.com/products-search/hot-china-products/Stand_Mixers.html).

### Cited Findings — freight China to Lagos
- Sino-Shipping (Sept/Oct 2026): **20GP $2,970–3,630; 40GP $3,150–3,850**; LCL $435/cbm; sea 35–38 days, LCL 36–42 days; rates may shift with Middle East conditions — [Sino-Shipping](https://www.sino-shipping.com/freight-china-nigeria/).
- Other 2026 LCL quotes: $150–195/CBM (1 CBM minimum, 40–45 days) — [Goodhope Freight](https://goodhopefreight.com/nigeria/lcl.html); $100–180/CBM, 45–50 days (Aug 2026) — [Tonlexing](https://www.tonlexing.com/sea-freight-from-china-to-nigeria/); $85–140 Lagos / $90–150 Apapa / $95–155 Tin Can — [Winsail](https://winsaillogistics.com/shipping-cost-per-cbm-from-china-to-nigeria/); $70–120 — [Viputrans](https://www.viputrans.com/sea-freight-from-china-to-nigeria.html); $180–320 (Jan 2026) — [Dantful](https://www.dantful.com/container-shipping-costs-from-china-to-nigeria/); "from $35/CBM DDP" (promotional) — [Dechaser](https://dechaser.com/china-to-nigeria-shipping/).
- CMA CGM peak season surcharge of $600/TEU on China–Nigeria from 15 Mar 2026 (current status unconfirmed) — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/?p=2801154).
- Ocean figures typically exclude origin handling, export customs, THC/CFS, documentation, destination THC, duty and delivery — [Goodhope via search](https://goodhopefreight.com/nigeria/2026-freight.html).
- The only naira figure seen: ₦430,000/CBM for solar products from a Lagos forwarder (unverified, product-specific) — via [Winsail/search summary](https://winsaillogistics.com/shipping-cost-per-cbm-from-china-to-nigeria/).

### Inferences
- Freight per unit is small for China LCL. Example: about 15–20 boxed air fryers per CBM at $100–200/CBM is roughly **$5–13/unit**, about ₦7k–17k. That is far below UK per-kg cargo for the same item (about ₦21k–37k). Nigerian "door-to-door" China agents commonly quote all-in per-CBM (customs included), which can absorb duty informally. The legality and receipt quality of such quotes vary.
- Export-platform MOQs (500+) are too high for a small importer. 1688 via a buying agent or Yiwu consolidator allows mixed small quantities but adds agent commission (not sourced here) and makes quality control the buyer's job.
- Consistency: one factory SKU can be reordered identically, so the same model, packaging and spares are available. This is the core advantage for resellers.

- No sourced agent commission rates (1688/Yiwu agents) or QC inspection fees.

## 6. Head-to-head: landed cost, supply consistency, brand perception, defect risk, fit for repeat-SKU resellers

### Takeaway
On landed cost per unit and repeatability, China wins clearly. UK liquidation wins only on brand authenticity ("original" Kenwood, Tefal and others) and on selling individual higher-value pieces to customers who pay a premium for "UK/London used" or tokunbo. UK supply is irregular, comes in mixed lots, carries defect risk, costs much more per kg to ship, and is legally sensitive if items are untested. For resellers who need the same SKU every month, China (or new branded stock) is the workable core. UK stock fits an opportunistic premium side line.

### Cited Findings
- "UK used" and "tokunbo" are established selling terms on Nigerian marketplaces. UK-origin used cookers, microwaves and fridges are listed at about ₦25,000–₦370,000 — [Jiji](https://jiji.ng/ibadan/kitchen-appliances/tokunbo-imported-uk-used-table-gas-cooker-8Qh8AuLgo3NZ6LMMeJ68tKhu.html); [OList](https://olist.ng/kitchen-appliances/UK-used-fridge-27516729491457.html).
- Nairaland used-goods importers (2016) reported that naira depreciation cut sales of used household items. They described warehouse stores selling blenders, cookers and cake mixers sourced from Asia, the UK and the USA — [Nairaland](https://www.nairaland.com/2861381/importer-used-foreign-goods-welcome); 2017 thread advertising UK-used appliances — [Nairaland](https://www.nairaland.com/3744509/where-get-uk-used-household).
- Tokunbo goods often have a shorter working life and reliability problems — [Wikipedia: Tokunbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokunbo_vehicle).
- Lagos vendors buy European equipment untested and sell it untested; about a quarter of studied used-EEE imports did not work — [Al Jazeera 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/3/27/truly-junk-e-waste-from-rich-nations-floods-local-markets-in-nigeria).

### Illustrative landed-cost logic (inference; all inputs flagged)
Item: mid-range air fryer (about 5.5kg gross, about 0.05 CBM boxed). FX: ₦1,331/$ (sourced), ₦1,770/£ (assumed).

| Step | UK grade-B return (e.g. Tower/Salter) | China new (1688 via agent) |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase | Assume RRP £70 × 40% ≈ £28 (≈ ₦49.6k) [43% from Stocklear; RRP assumed] | ¥113 ≈ $16 at an assumed ¥7.1/$ ≈ ₦21k (agent fee extra, unsourced) |
| UK/China local collection, testing, packing | Unknown; assume £3–5 | Included in agent fee (assumed) |
| Sea freight to Lagos | 5.5kg × £2.50–3.50 = £14–19 (≈ ₦24k–34k) | 0.05 CBM × $100–195 = $5–10 (≈ ₦7k–13k) |
| Duty + VAT + levies (assume ~31% of CIF, 20% band) | ≈ ₦23k–27k if cleared formally (often bundled "all-inclusive" by diaspora cargo firms) | ≈ ₦9k–11k |
| SONCAP (spread over a shipment) | $350+ per shipment, a heavy share of a small mixed lot | $350 per shipment plus a registered PC, spread over hundreds of units |
| Write-off for defects | Unknown; Al Jazeera's 25% non-functioning figure is for used EEE broadly, so assume 10–25% for untested returns, less for graded B | Factory DOA typically lower; unsourced |
| **Indicative landed cost** | **≈ ₦100k–120k per working unit** | **≈ ₦40k–50k per unit** |

- Read this table as about 2–2.5× cheaper per unit from China for a generic air fryer. The UK unit has to sell as a branded "original" or UK-used product at a premium to compete.
- For heavy, high-value branded items (a Kenwood stand mixer), the per-kg freight is a smaller share of value, so the UK route is relatively less bad. For light, cheap items (utensils, bakeware) the per-kg rate matters less, but China's unit price advantage dominates.

### Inferences
- **Supply consistency:** UK lots are one-off manifests with mixed SKUs, colours and conditions, so you cannot promise a reseller "50 of model X every month". China supplies identical SKUs on reorder.
- **Brand perception:** Nigerian buyers pay for "original"/UK brands and distrust generic "China" goods. But many UK-brand appliances (Tower, Salter, Russell Hobbs, Swan) are themselves made in China, so the premium is for the brand and QC, not the origin. The importer can also commission UK-plug, own-brand or OEM goods from Chinese factories.
- **Return/defect risk:** Higher with UK returns (missing parts, DOA, recalls). China's risk is batch-quality variance, which inspection reduces.
- **Best fit:** China (or authorised distributors of new branded stock) for repeat SKUs. UK liquidation as opportunistic premium stock (branded stand mixers, cookware sets) sold item by item at retail.

### Gaps
- No sourced Nigerian retail price comparison for new China air fryers or mixers versus UK-used equivalents.
- No sourced consumer survey on Nigerian brand perception of kitchen appliances.

## 7. Hybrid models used by others (diaspora barrels/containers)

### Takeaway
A large UK–Nigeria diaspora cargo industry (Peckham, Tilbury-based consolidators) ships boxes, barrels and pallets by weight, weekly or monthly, often "all-inclusive" of Nigerian clearing. This is the practical route for small UK sourcing. The common hybrid is UK-based family or agents buying clearance lots, testing them, and shipping via these consolidators alongside personal cargo.

### Cited Findings
- Diaspora cargo operators with published per-kg sea rates and minimums: R-Zone (weekly), CargoAfrik (Tilbury, roughly monthly), A-B Cargo Express, Hezelinks, Cargonaija, Geeomotayo — [CargoAfrik](https://cargoafrik.co.uk/price-list-of-cargo-to-nigeria/); [A-B Cargo](http://www.abcargoexpress.com/a-bce/); [Hezelinks](https://hezelinks.com/sea-freight-cargo-shipping-from-uk-to-nigeria/); [Cargonaija](https://cargonaija.com/cargo-from-uk-to-nigeria/); [Geeomotayo](https://www.geeomotayo.com/price-list-uk-to-nigeria/).
- Used goods (including e-waste) also enter Nigeria hidden inside used vehicles shipped from Europe — [Phys.org 2018](https://phys.org/news/2018-04-thousands-tons-e-waste-shipped-illegally.html). This is a non-compliant practice, not a model to copy.
- Nigerian importers also mix sources: one used-goods warehouse sold blenders and cake mixers from Asia, the UK and the USA — [Nairaland](https://www.nairaland.com/2861381/importer-used-foreign-goods-welcome).

### Inferences
- A sensible hybrid for a small importer: (1) a China core of repeat SKUs (mixers, hand mixers, bakeware, cookware, commercial fryers) via a Lagos-based China agent or own-account LCL; (2) a UK side line of tested, branded clearance pieces, bought by a UK-based contact from resellers or auctions and shipped by per-kg diaspora cargo, sold as "UK original" at premium prices; (3) once volume justifies it (more than about 15 CBM), move UK buys into a consolidated 20ft with formal SONCAP and Form M clearing.
- Barrel and box shipping keeps legal exposure low only if goods are tested and working, and declared commercially when quantities are commercial.

### Gaps
- No YouTube or blog case studies of UK–Nigeria kitchen-appliance resellers with real margins were found in this session.
- No data on how diaspora "all-inclusive" cargo firms handle duty, SONCAP and receipts for commercial goods.
