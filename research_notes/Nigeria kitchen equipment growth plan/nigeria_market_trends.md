# Nigeria Kitchen, Baking and Food-Business Equipment: Market Direction 2025-2026

Research date: 9 Oct 2026. Notes for a small Lagos importer (Chinese stand mixers sold one-to-one, locally made baking pans, brokered deck ovens and stainless work tables) deciding whether to stay in baking or broaden. Older data is flagged inline. A lot of the evidence is thin or secondary, and that is flagged too.

## 1. Macro context 2025-2026: naira, inflation, spending power, and what it means for appliance and baking demand

### Takeaway
The macro picture improved on paper through 2026. Inflation is down to about 15.4% (rebased CPI), the naira has held roughly in the N1,370-1,425/$ range and is about 13% stronger year on year, and the CBN cut its policy rate to 23% in Sept 2026. Households still feel squeezed, though. Consumer sentiment fell sharply in Sept 2026 and buying intentions for appliances are deeply negative, so spending goes to essentials (food) over discretionary items (celebration cakes, new appliances).

### Cited Findings
**Inflation**
- August 2026 headline inflation was 15.39% y/y, down from 15.43% in July and from 23.14% in August 2025. BusinessDay reported 15.41%, so sources differ slightly. Food, transport and housing contributed 9.10 percentage points. — [VON](https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-headline-inflation-eases-to-15-39-in-august-nbs/); [Gazette NG](https://gazettengr.com/nigerias-headline-inflation-falls-to-15-43/)
- August 2026 food inflation was 19.57% y/y (25.30% in Aug 2025). Food m/m fell to 1.02% from 5.56% in July 2026, so July 2026 had a sharp food-price jump. — [VON](https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-headline-inflation-eases-to-15-39-in-august-nbs/)
- The CPI was rebased: 2024 is the base year and 2023 the weight reference period. Comparisons with pre-rebase figures (for example the 30%+ readings of 2024) are not like-for-like. — [VON](https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-headline-inflation-eases-to-15-39-in-august-nbs/)
- January 2026 headline inflation was 15.10%. — [Nairametrics MPR explainer](https://nairametrics.com/2026/02/25/explainer-three-reasons-why-the-cbn-cut-mpr-rates-to-26-5/)
- June 2026 reporting had inflation at 15.93% "amid high food prices". — [AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202606160021.html)
- September 2026 CPI had not been published as of 9 Oct 2026.

**Exchange rate**
- The naira closed May 2026 at about N1,372/$, against N1,367 at end-April. That is a 13.5% (N213.50) appreciation year on year. The article was found on a Nairametrics staging domain. — [Nairametrics](https://nm-stag.nairametrics.com/naira-weakens-against-dollar-closes-may-at-n1372/)
- Mid-May 2026 the official rate was about N1,371/$. Analysts credit CBN interventions and better dollar supply to authorised dealers with reducing volatility. — [Channels TV](https://www.channelstv.com/?p=1026606)
- Stability is attributed to tight monetary policy, higher oil output and stronger FX inflows. — [Nigeria Housing Market](https://www.nigeriahousingmarket.com/news/naira-holds-firm-cbn-hawkish-policy-oil-output-fx-stability)
- SBM Intelligence forecast for 2026 was N1,470-1,520/$. Actual trading has been stronger than that. — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/cbn-expected-to-prioritise-naira-stability-over-appreciation-in-2026/)
- A low-quality aggregator puts the rate at about N1,418-1,425 later in 2026. This is not verified against CBN. — [Nigeria Housing Market tracker](https://www.nigeriahousingmarket.com/guides/central-bank-of-nigeria-exchange-rate-today)

**Interest rates**
- The MPR was cut 50bp to 26.5% in Feb 2026, citing stable FX and capital inflows. — [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/02/25/explainer-three-reasons-why-the-cbn-cut-mpr-rates-to-26-5/)
- The MPR was cut 350bp to 23% at the 307th MPC on 22 Sept 2026. — [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/22/cbn-resets-mpr-to-23/); [Arise TV](https://www.arise.tv/cbn-cuts-mpr-to-23-from-26-5-as-mpc-revises-policy/)

**Consumer spending power**
- CBN Household Expectations Survey: the Consumer Sentiment Index fell to -18.7 in Sept 2026 from -9.9 in August. The Buying Intention Index was 16.6, well below the 50 neutral line. Households are putting money into necessities and postponing less urgent spending. — [BrandSpur](https://brandspurng.com/2026/10/05/nigerias-consumer-confidence-falls-to-18-7-points-as-inflation-fears-deepen/)
- The share of households perceiving inflation as high rose to 77.2% in Sept 2026 from 67.2% in August. — [BrandSpur](https://brandspurng.com/2026/10/05/nigerias-consumer-confidence-falls-to-18-7-points-as-inflation-fears-deepen/)
- The CBN survey (March 2026) showed deeply negative buying sentiment for housing, vehicles and appliances. — [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/07/nigerians-suspend-home-car-purchases-as-living-costs-squeeze-households/); [Nairametrics Oct 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/04/cbn-nigerians-suspend-investments-house-purchases-as-living-costs-bite-harder/)
- BCG Africa Consumer Sentiment Survey 2025: 83% of Nigerian households are cutting discretionary purchases. Essentials (food, housing, utilities, transport) take nearly three-quarters of household budgets. — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/?p=1066360)
- Euromonitor Nigeria Consumer Profile (July 2026) says discretionary spending remains well below the regional average after subsidy removal and devaluation. This is from the summary only; the report is paywalled. — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/nigeria-consumer-profile/report)

**Energy costs (relevant to gas fryers, cookers and ovens)**
- NBS: the average 12.5kg LPG refill was about N22,382 in April 2026, up about 13.9% m/m from about N19,653 in March and about 10.4% y/y. NALPGAM quotes N1,500-1,700/kg and blames supply shortages. — [Gazette NG](https://gazettengr.com/tinubu-hardship-prices-of-cooking-gas-continue-to-rise-in-nigeria/); [Leadership](https://leadership.ng/?p=589142)

### Inferences
- Importers face a better environment than in 2023-24. A stable and slightly stronger naira lowers landed cost and the risk of repricing stock. The rate cut may ease credit for small businesses buying equipment, but that will take time to show.
- Household demand is the weak point. Buying intentions for appliances are below neutral and falling. Sales that depend on households making discretionary purchases, such as a home baker's first stand mixer or a hobby-baking setup, face headwinds. Purchases that earn money (equipment for a food business that sells daily necessities) are easier to justify for buyers.
- Food is a necessity and food inflation is still about 19.6%. Vendors selling everyday food have steadier demand than celebration-cake makers.
- Rising LPG prices hurt gas-equipment operating costs. They may also push buyers toward fuel-efficient fryers and cookers, which is a possible selling point.

### Gaps
- Official CBN NFEM rate for Sept/Oct 2026 not confirmed. Figures above are from news and aggregators.
- September 2026 CPI not yet released.
- No data found on real wage or income growth in 2026.
- No data found on LPG prices for May-Sept 2026.

## 2. Is the Nigerian cake and home-baking business slowing or saturating?

### Takeaway
There is consistent anecdotal and association-level evidence (mostly 2023-2025) that bakers face high input costs and weaker demand for celebration cakes. Flour was still about N55,000-62,000 per 50kg bag in Dec 2025, with no confirmed 2026 fall. I found no hard data on the number of home bakers or on saturation. Saturation is plausible but undocumented.

### Cited Findings
- Dec 2025: the Premium Breadmakers Association of Nigeria (PBAN) called claims of flour falling to N35,000-40,000 false. It put the 50kg bag at N55,000-62,000 depending on brand and location. Some millers had cut about N2,000, which PBAN called "a drop in the ocean". Bakers refused to cut bread prices. — [AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202512180129.html); [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/agriculture/article/bakers-dismiss-flour-price-reduction-claim/)
- BusinessDay reported an average flour price of about N59,000/bag (late 2025). — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/agriculture/article/bakers-dismiss-flour-price-reduction-claim/)
- Kano: after a government-brokered deal, bakers bought flour at about N60,000/bag and increased loaf weight rather than cutting prices. — [Milling MEA](https://millingmea.com/nigerias-kano-government-bakers-association-agree-to-increase-size-and-quality-of-bread-price-remains-unchanged/)
- Bakers protested soaring flour prices despite a duty-free import waiver on wheat/food items (2024-25). — [Milling MEA](https://millingmea.com/nigerian-bakers-protest-soaring-flour-prices-despite-duty-free-import-waiver/)
- An Abuja baker said customers stopped ordering birthday cakes, especially for children, and buy biscuits and drinks instead. Valentine's orders fell sharply. The article date was not confirmed (likely 2024), and the page could not be fetched. — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/news/nigeria/bakers-decry-low-patronage-of-pastries-amidst-high-cost-of-materials/)
- An FCT trader quoted flour rising from N35,000 (Dec 2023) to N46,000-48,000, sugar from N62,000 to N75,000-80,000 per bag, and butter from N23,000 to N28,000 per carton in under two months. This is early 2024 data. — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/news/nigeria/bakers-decry-low-patronage-of-pastries-amidst-high-cost-of-materials/)
- AMBCN (Association of Master Bakers and Caterers of Nigeria) protested in Feb 2024 to "save the collapsing baking industry", citing flour, sugar, yeast, oil, petrol and diesel costs. It asked for import liberalisation and tax relief. — [Milling MEA](https://millingmea.com/nigerian-bakers-and-caterers-protest-to-save-the-collapsing-baking-industry/)
- Lagos bakeries rely on diesel generators because of erratic power, and many run below installed capacity. This is older data. — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/energy/article/nigerian-bakers-suffer-as-power-supply-impacts-business/)
- Reports of bread scarcity in Kaduna, Kano and Katsina. The page carries a 2026 header date, but the content appears to be from 2024. — [Gazette NG](https://gazettengr.com/tinubus-inflation-bread-scarcity-hits-kaduna-kano-katsina/)

### Inferences
- Celebration cakes are the most discretionary baked product, and they are hit by both high costs and squeezed customers. Bread is a staple and holds demand better, but margins are thin.
- Bakers facing high flour costs and generator fuel are less likely to buy new stand mixers. Demand for replacements, repairs and second-hand equipment may hold up better than demand for new kit.
- Deck ovens and dough mixers for bread bakeries serve a necessity product, so they are probably more resilient than home-baker mixers. This is inferred from the necessity-vs-discretionary pattern, not measured.

### Gaps
- No 2026 flour, sugar, butter or egg price series found. The latest flour price is Dec 2025.
- No data on the number of home or small bakers, baking-class enrolment, or Nairaland/Instagram sentiment on saturation. (Nairaland was not reached in this search budget.)
- No sales data on stand mixers in Nigeria.

## 3. Which food micro-businesses are growing, and what equipment do they buy?

### Takeaway
Small chops, street food and food delivery show steady demand even during the cost-of-living crisis, because they are affordable and tied to events and commuting. The evidence is mostly qualitative and partly older. Equipment that shows up repeatedly in listings and sourcing requests: gas and electric fryers, shawarma machines, bain-marie and food warmers, griddles, popcorn machines, blenders and freezers. No market-size figures exist for these segments.

### Cited Findings
- Small chops are standard at weddings, birthdays, seminars and corporate events, a niche for caterers. An Ogun vendor says the business stays profitable despite flour, oil and egg price rises "because these snacks are affordable". The date is not confirmed. — [Leadership](https://leadership.ng/?p=633623)
- Smallchopsng (Lagos) reported N100m revenue over seven years. This is 2023 data and shows a shift to delivery and online ordering. — [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/2023/08/21/smallchopsng-100-million-selling-small-chops/)
- smallChops (finger-food maker) planned expansion beyond Lagos after post-COVID growth. This is older data. — [Food Business MEA](https://www.foodbusinessmea.com/nigerian-finger-foods-maker-smallchops-plans-expansion-beyond-lagos-after-strong-post-covid-growth/)
- Street food in Lagos "defies cash crunch". Many people, including unemployed graduates, are entering street-food vending (c. 2023). — [Daily Trust](https://dailytrust.com/how-street-food-business-defies-cash-crunch-in-lagos/)
- An increasing majority of the urban poor get their daily nutrition from street food, which drives expansion of informal ready-to-eat vending (academic, 2022-23). — [Frontiers](https://frontiersin.org/journals/sustainable-food-systems/articles/10.3389/fsufs.2023.1160156/text)
- Food delivery and running your own kitchen are listed as Lagos side hustles (May 2025). — [Discover Lagos](https://www.discoverlagos.ng/post/profitable-side-hustles-you-can-start-in-lagos)
- Lagos foodpreneur scale-ups: one vendor reportedly serves 4,000+ packs a day across three locations, and others have multi-branch operations in Yaba, Lekki and Ikeja. This is a promotional source. — [FoodBay TV](https://foodbay.tv/from-side-hustle-to-street-king-the-rise-of-nigerias-foodpreneurs/)

**Equipment prices and sourcing signals**
- Shawarma machine prices in Nigeria (2026 guide): table-top N200k-350k, traditional vertical N150k-300k, dedicated multi-level N500k-800k, single-person units N90k-150k. — [Nigerian Queries](https://nigerianqueries.com/sharwama-machine-prices-in-nigeria/)
- Locally fabricated stainless gas shawarma toaster grills are sold in Lagos ("Made in Nigeria by skilled fabricators"). This is local competition for imported units. — [Jiji listing](https://jiji.ng/mushin/restaurant-and-catering-equipment/fryer-with-foreign-burner-e1vrjCXQZ6b9KKLJWxkkTU2W.html)
- An import buyer request for CIF Lagos covers an electric fryer, electric griddle, bain-marie, and gas and electric shawarma machines, 220-240V/50Hz, CE/ISO preferred. These products are being sourced as a bundle. — [Freshdi](https://freshdi.com/request/Cooking-Hot-Food-Equipment)
- Jiji dealer listings bundle ice-cube machines, deep fryers, barbecue machines and popcorn machines, which shows a commercial equipment segment exists on the platform. — [Jiji](https://jiji.ng/kitchen-appliances/nikai)

### Inferences
- Food micro-businesses selling affordable everyday or event food (small chops, shawarma, puff-puff, street meals) look more resilient than celebration cakes. They also buy a wider range of equipment, which suits a broker or importer.
- The fryer + shawarma + bain-marie + griddle bundle suggests a "starter kit" opportunity: package equipment for new vendors (fryer + food warmer + chest freezer + blender).
- Local fabricators compete on shawarma grills, chin chin cutters and fryers. Imported value has to come from quality, thermostats or certification, or from price on items local fabricators can't make well, such as electric fryers and industrial blenders.

### Gaps
- No market-size or growth data for small chops, shawarma, catering, cloud kitchens, chin chin, zobo/yogurt, popcorn or puff-puff in 2025-26.
- No price data found for gas fryers, chin chin cutters, industrial blenders, sealing machines or bain-maries in Nigeria.
- No Nigeria-specific cloud-kitchen data for 2025-26.

## 4. Household kitchenware demand: what moves in volume?

### Takeaway
Blenders are the clearest volume category, and air fryers are the growth niche (Euromonitor 2025). On Jiji, ad counts are dominated by refrigerators, freezers and cookers, followed by microwaves, blenders and air fryers. Food flasks are sold heavily for events, church programmes, food vendors and souvenirs. I found no hard volume data on pots or cookware sets. Local aluminium pot makers, both formal and artisanal, are part of the pot market.

### Cited Findings
- Euromonitor Small Cooking Appliances in Nigeria (2025): air fryers and social-media marketing drive "modest growth amid economic constraints". Freestanding hobs lead the category, Haier is gaining share, and Scanfrost is under pressure. This is summary-level only. — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/small-cooking-appliances-in-nigeria/report)
- Euromonitor Consumer Appliances in Nigeria (2025): health consciousness and working lifestyles drive time-saving devices, with a chart titled "Influencer Marketing Drives Blender Demand in Nigeria". Offline channels still dominate blender sales. — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/consumer-appliances-in-nigeria/report)
- Blenders and food processors saw slight volume growth in 2024. — [Research and Markets / Euromonitor](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/4436315/food-preparation-appliances-in-nigeria)
- Statista (via EqualOcean, June 2025): kitchen appliances are 17.38% of home-goods sales on Jumia, with blenders in Nigeria named as particularly popular. The source does not say whether this figure is Nigeria-only. — [EqualOcean](https://equalocean.com/news/2025060621562-statista-african-small-appliance-market-will-reach-usd-26-03-bn-2025)
- Jiji kitchen-appliance ad counts (supply, not demand): refrigerators 29,820; freezers 22,567; cookers 19,738; microwaves 7,426; blenders 7,171; air fryers 5,142. — [Jiji](https://jiji.ng/kitchen-appliances/nikai)
- Food flasks on Jiji (Lagos Island/Okearin Market) are marketed for homes, events, church programmes, outdoor catering and food vendors. Souvenir double-wall flasks are listed in Ikeja. — [Jiji 2L flask](https://jiji.ng/ha/lagos-island-west/kitchen-and-dining/2l-food-flask-BXfppilcoAXQqmTpbanBFBek.html); [Jiji souvenir flask](https://jiji.ng/ha/ikeja/kitchen-and-dining/souvenir-insulated-double-wall-flask-BJPvNU8YjGW617NH0OT35oNP.html)
- Lagos gifting and souvenir vendors (Giftwareaffair, SipCo, TheKitchenAffairs_ng) offer event, wedding and kitchen souvenirs. This is vendor marketing. — [ConnectCiti](https://connectciti.com/listing/giftwareaffair); [SipCo](https://sipco-landing-page.lovable.app/)
- Local cookware makers: Tower Aluminium makes pots and pans for Nigeria and West Africa, and Louis Carter (Nnewi) makes cookware. — [Odua Investment](https://oduainvestment.com.ng/tower-aluminium-nigeria-limited); [AllBusiness Africa](https://allbusiness.africa/business/louis-carter-industries)
- Artisanal recycled-aluminium pot production (e.g., Saki) is economically significant. Informal artisans melt scrap into pots and spoons, notably for cooking at weddings and birthdays. These are 2020-era sources. — [PolyU paper](https://research.polyu.edu.hk/en/publications/evaluation-of-indigenous-technology-for-cast-aluminium-cookware-p/); [PIND brief](https://pindfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Recycling-Strategy-Brief.pdf)

### Inferences
- Blenders sit in both the household and food-business segments (zobo, pepper, smoothies, puff-puff batter), which makes them a low-risk adjacent category to a stand-mixer line. Industrial and commercial blenders bridge into food business.
- Flasks and coolers serve owambe, souvenir and vendor demand. Bulk souvenir orders could offer volume in a way one-to-one mixer sales do not, but this is unquantified.
- Large pots and cookware face strong local competition, both formal and artisanal recycled aluminium. Imported non-stick or stainless sets would compete at the mid-to-premium end.

### Gaps
- No volume or price data for non-stick sets, stainless pots, storage containers or utensils in 2025-26.
- No data on the size of the owambe, bridal-shower or souvenir gifting market, or on its sensitivity to the cost-of-living squeeze.

## 5. Government and policy: duties, levies, restrictions, local manufacturing

### Takeaway
Consumer goods such as appliances and cookware generally fall in the 20% ECOWAS CET band, plus VAT and other charges. Line-level HS rates were not confirmed. The 4% FOB levy was implemented in Aug 2025 and suspended in Sept 2025; I found no evidence of reinstatement. No specific import ban on kitchen appliances or cookware was found.

### Cited Findings
- Nigeria applies five ECOWAS CET bands, including 5% for essentials, raw materials and capital goods, and 20% for consumer goods. Some 80+ tariff lines carry effective duty of 50%+ (tariff + levy + excise + VAT). These lines were not identified as appliances or cookware. — [Proshare / USTR 2025 NTE](https://proshare.co/articles/2025-national-trade-estimate-report-on-foreign-trade-barriers); [trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/knowledge-product/nigeria-import-tariffs)
- Nigeria Customs migrated to the new ECOWAS CET in April 2022. — [trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/knowledge-product/nigeria-import-tariffs)
- 4% FOB charge: announced and then suspended in Feb 2025, implemented from 4 Aug 2025, and suspended again by Finance Minister Wale Edun's circular of 15 Sept 2025 over inflation and trade-facilitation concerns. — [AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202509160081.html); [Orbitax](https://orbitax.com/news/archive.php/Nigeria-Implements-and-Suspend-58146)
- Exemptions from the 4% FOB applied to manufacturers' raw materials and machinery under Customs Tariff Chapters 98/99. — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/maritime/article/full-list-of-import-goods-exempted-from-customs-4-fob-levy/)
- A 1% CISS fee applies separately. — [Daily Trust](https://dailytrust.com/4-fob-charge-needless-burden-on-nigerians/)
- Form M: the CBN directed (2024) that the closing FX rate on the day Form M is opened be used for duty assessment, to reduce duty-rate volatility. This is 2024 data. — search summary of CBN circular (no direct URL retrieved)
- The government has reviewed the import prohibition list in the past. No current kitchenware ban was found. — [Templars](https://www.templars-law.com/news/federal-government-reviews-import-prohibition-regime/)

### Inferences
- Commercial food equipment, such as deck ovens and industrial mixers, might qualify as capital goods (5% band) while household appliances sit at 20%. This is worth checking by HS code because it could favour the commercial-equipment side.
- A stable naira and the suspended 4% FOB charge make 2026 landed costs more predictable than in 2024-25.

### Gaps
- No HS-line duty rates confirmed (e.g., 8509 blenders/mixers, 7323 steel cookware, 7615 aluminium cookware, 8419/8516 ovens and fryers).
- No 2026 information on whether the 4% FOB levy was reinstated, or on any new FX-access rules for importers.
- No SON/SONCAP certification cost data found.

## 6. Key marketplaces and trade hubs

### Takeaway
Jiji carries a large stock of kitchen-appliance listings and an active commercial restaurant-equipment segment. Jumia was the leading online appliance store in Nigeria in 2024, but online is small next to offline. No bestseller lists for Jumia or Jiji were found. I found no 2025-26 reporting on Trade Fair, Alaba, Idumota or Lagos Island kitchenware trade, except that Jiji flask sellers are based in Okearin Market, Lagos Island.

### Cited Findings
- jumia.com.ng generated about US$6.7m revenue in Nigeria's electrical-appliance e-commerce market in 2024, making it the leading online store. — [ecommerceDB](https://ecommercedb.com/markets/ng/major-appliances)
- Euromonitor: online sales are growing, but offline channels still dominate blender sales. — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/consumer-appliances-in-nigeria/report)
- Jiji listing counts by category (see Q4). Jiji also hosts restaurant and catering equipment dealers in Mushin and elsewhere. — [Jiji](https://jiji.ng/kitchen-appliances/nikai); [Jiji Mushin](https://jiji.ng/mushin/restaurant-and-catering-equipment/fryer-with-foreign-burner-e1vrjCXQZ6b9KKLJWxkkTU2W.html)
- Influencer and social-media marketing are a key driver of air-fryer and blender demand. — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/small-cooking-appliances-in-nigeria/report)
- Lagos kitchenware and souvenir vendors sell through Instagram and online listings (e.g., TheKitchenAffairs_ng). — [ConnectCiti](https://connectciti.com/listing/giftwareaffair)

### Inferences
- Instagram and WhatsApp with influencer content remain the main demand-creation channel for small appliances. That fits the importer's current one-to-one model, and the same channel could carry a broader range.
- Jiji is a useful free signal of competitive density. Air fryers and blenders already have thousands of listings, so the importer would be entering a crowded market.

### Gaps
- No Jumia or Jiji bestseller or trend reports specific to kitchen in 2025-26.
- No reporting found on Trade Fair, Alaba, Idumota or Lagos Island kitchen-equipment trade volumes or prices.
- Nairaland threads were not reviewed within the search budget.
